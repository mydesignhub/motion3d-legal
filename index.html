Motion 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Motion 3D is published by MyDesign Studio ("we"). This policy covers the Motion 3D app for Android.

Summary

Motion 3D is a video editor that works on your device. Editing, previewing and exporting all happen on the device. It has no accounts and no ads. It does not collect your projects, photos, videos, recordings or text. They stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself. When the app crashes it sends a crash report so we can fix it; you can turn that off.

What stays on your device

  • The photos, videos and audio you import, the projects you make and the videos you export.
  • Voice recordings. The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice-over.
  • Auto Subtitle (speech to text), background removal, text to speech and the other AI tools. They run on your device and do not upload your media.
  • The app's error log. It is kept on the device and is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to show your gallery inside the app so you can import media, and to save your exports to the gallery. On Android 14 and later you can give access to selected items only.
  • Microphone: to record a voice-over, only when you start a recording.
  • Internet: for the downloads, crash reports and purchases described below.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Motion 3D Pro.
  • Keep awake: so the phone does not sleep while a video is exporting.

Downloads the app makes

Some features download files, the first time you use them or when the app checks for an update to them. Like any internet request, a download sends your IP address and basic device and app information to the server that hosts the file. It never contains your media or projects.

  • Speech recognition models for Auto Subtitle, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy
  • Fonts and animated emoji stickers, from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com). Privacy information: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy
  • 3D text presets, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The app checks this list when it starts. Privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Stock photos and videos, in versions of the app that include online search, from Pixabay, Pexels and Unsplash. The words you search for are sent to that service. Their policies: https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/ and https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/ and https://unsplash.com/privacy

Crash reports

When Motion 3D crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the error and where in the app's code it happened, the device model and operating system version, the app's version, the time, and a random identifier for this installation that does not identify you. With crash reports on, the app also tells Firebase when it is opened, with the same device and app details, so we can see what share of sessions end in a crash. None of this contains your videos, photos, recordings, projects or text. You can turn crash reports off in Settings → Send crash reports. Google's privacy information for Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google ML Kit

Background removal for people uses Google ML Kit. ML Kit processes your images on the device and does not send them to Google. It does send Google diagnostic and usage information: the device model and operating system version, this app's name and version, performance figures such as how long a detection took, which features were used, and a random identifier for this installation that does not identify you or your device. Google's terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Text to speech

Voice-over uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device, usually Google's. If your engine uses an online voice, it may send the text to its provider under that provider's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Motion 3D Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro, and keeps that answer on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. When you share an export, it goes to the app you choose, under that app's policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: projects, the media copied into them, exports, downloaded models and fonts stay until you remove them. You can delete a project in the app, clear temporary files in Settings → Clear cache, and remove everything the app stores by uninstalling it. Exports already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them.
  • Device backup: if backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include some of the app's data in the backup kept in your own Google account. You control that in your device settings.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them. Turning off Settings → Send crash reports stops new ones.
  • We have no accounts and no server that holds your content, so we hold nothing else about you. If you have a question or a request about your data, write to the address below.

Children

Motion 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
