Motion 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Summary

Motion 3D is a video editor that works on your device. It has no accounts and no ads. It does not collect your projects, photos, videos, recordings or text. They stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

What stays on your device

  • The photos, videos and audio you import, the projects you make and the videos you export.
  • Voice recordings. The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice-over.
  • Auto Subtitle (speech to text), background removal, text to speech and the other AI tools. They run on your device and do not upload your media.
  • The app's error log. It is kept on the device and is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to import media and to save your exports to the gallery.
  • Microphone: to record a voice-over, only when you start a recording.

Downloads the app makes

Some features download files the first time you use them. Like any internet request, a download sends your IP address and basic device and app information to the server that hosts the file. It never contains your media or projects.

  • Speech recognition models for Auto Subtitle, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co).
  • Fonts and animated emoji stickers, from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com).
  • 3D text presets, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • Stock photos and videos you search for, from Pixabay, Pexels and Unsplash. The words you search for are sent to that service.

Google ML Kit

Background removal for people uses Google ML Kit. ML Kit processes your images on the device and does not send them to Google. It does send Google diagnostic and usage information: the device model and operating system version, this app's name and version, performance figures such as how long a detection took, which features were used, and a random identifier for this installation that does not identify you or your device. Google's terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Text to speech

Voice-over uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device, usually Google's. If your engine uses an online voice, it may send the text to its provider under that provider's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Motion 3D Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro, and keeps that answer on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. When you share an export, it goes to the app you choose, under that app's policy.

Children

Motion 3D is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
